What is Agora?

The realtime platform connecting people, devices, and AI.

Realtime media

Voice, video, and live streaming

Agora Channels create the shared live space where participants can hear, see, and respond to one another.

Global delivery

Built for conversations that feel immediate

Agora’s software-defined realtime network routes live media for quality, reliability, and scale.

Conversational AI

Agents become realtime participants

Agora orchestrates voice agents that can join a Channel, listen, reason, speak, and respond on demand.

Powering 1 Trillion minutes of live voice and video streams per year.

You build the experience. Agora carries the live conversation.

Thirty seconds. Channels are the shared space, SD-RTN delivers the media, and the Conversational AI Engine brings Gemini into that space as a participant.

Two Gemini stages in the cascade. Agora keeps the conversation moving in realtime.

Follow the top row left to right: the user publishes audio; Gemini 3.5 Transcribe Live produces the transcript; Gemini 3.6 Flash produces the response; Agora-managed TTS synthesizes speech.

Then follow the return path: the agent publishes audio back into the same Channel. This is the cascaded recipe you are about to run. Next slide shows the same loop with Gemini Live.

Agora SD-RTN®The underlying realtime network · one path, both directions · runs the agent session
Audio in · device → Gemini Live Audio out · Gemini Live → device

Same Channel, same session lifecycle. The cascade collapses into one provider.

One network line, both directions: audio drops from the device into SD-RTN, crosses to Gemini Live, and the reply comes back along the same line to the device. SD-RTN is the layer underneath both endpoints; there is no text in the middle.

Trade-off in one sentence: the cascade gives you per-stage control and swappable vendors; native gives you the lowest hop count and the model’s own turn handling.
